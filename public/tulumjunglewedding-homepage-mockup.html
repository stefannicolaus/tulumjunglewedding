Casa Arkaana, Tulum
Conscious Unions

Celebrate Love in the Heart of the Mayan Jungle.

At Casa Arkaana, a wedding is more than a day — it is a sacred journey of love that can be as soulful, stylish, or wildly creative as you dream it to be. Some couples choose the intimacy of a retreat-style gathering, with yoga, cacao, and quiet moments of connection. Others embrace the free-spirited energy of a boho jungle celebration, adorned with woven lamps, flowers, and candlelight.

And for those who wish, the night can carry the vibrant pulse of a festival under the stars, with music, lights, and dancing deep into the jungle night.

Our off-grid sanctuary near Tulum offers a canvas where all these visions are possible — rooted in nature, supported by community, and designed with intention. With on-site accommodations for your closest loved ones, your celebration unfolds across days, giving space for arrival, ceremony, and integration.

Environmental Stewardship

Celebrate with Love — for Each Other and the Earth.

At Casa Arkaana, every wedding becomes part of a larger story — one where love is celebrated in harmony with the land that holds us. Beneath the trees and under the stars, we honour both people and planet by designing celebrations that leave only joy behind, never waste.

We compost, recycle, and tread lightly, ensuring that what nourishes your wedding also nourishes the Earth. At one jungle wedding, three days of festivities left behind only two small bags of trash — the rest returned to the soil or was given a new life.

Common Questions

Before you reach out.

The questions we hear most often.

How many guests can we invite?

Casa Arkaana hosts intimate weddings of 20–60 guests. We don't do large-scale events — every wedding here is personal. The space is designed for people who actually know each other.

Is Tulum safe for a destination wedding?

We hear this question often. Our guests travel from across the world. Casa Arkaana is a gated private property, 20 minutes from Tulum town, surrounded by jungle. "We spent three days there, enjoying the jungle, and left feeling renewed." — B & A, 2024. The setting is remote by design — that's what makes it what it is.

What's not included in the package?

Catering, photography, and your officiant are not included in the base package — we can connect you with trusted local vendors who know the space well. Everything that is included is listed above.

Can we arrive before the wedding day?

Yes. The 3-night accommodation is included — many couples arrive a day early to settle in, explore the cenote pool, and breathe. Arriving calm changes everything about a wedding day.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 6–12 months in advance. We accept only a limited number of weddings per year — this is our home, not a venue calendar. If you have a date in mind, reach out early.
