Section 00

Design Principles

Five binding rules. Every design decision traces back to one of these. When in doubt — subtract, don't add.

R01
White Space
White is not empty — it is room to breathe.
The page is predominantly white/cream. Negative space is a design element, not a gap to fill. Sections need air between them. Resist the urge to add.
R02
Typography
Serif carries emotion. Sans carries clarity.
Cormorant Garamond (weight 300) for headlines, quotes, and moments of feeling. DM Sans (weight 300) for body, labels, and navigation. Never mix roles.
R03
Dark Green
Jungle green is a pause — not a background.
Use #1D3828 for the hero (photo replacement only) and maximum one accent section. Never paint entire pages green. It should feel like stepping into shadow — rare, intentional.
R04
Gold
Gold is an underline, never a surface.
#B5904E appears as thin lines, eyebrow labels, and the occasional quote mark. It never fills a button, background, or card. Its restraint is what makes it feel expensive.
R05
Image Hierarchy
No element competes with the photograph.
When a photo is present, type steps back: weight 300, generous margins, minimal overlays. The jungle speaks. The design holds the frame.
Section 01

Color Palette

Warm White
#FAFAF7
Base background. All sections default to this. Warm enough to feel organic — not sterile.
BG — all text passes AA
Near Black
#1A1A18
Primary text. Softer than pure black — reads warm on cream backgrounds. Never use #000000.
On #FAFAF7: 17.5:1 — AAA
Jungle Green
#1D3828
Hero background (photo placeholder), primary buttons, max 1 dark section. Deep, satt — rainforest at night, not lime.
On #FAFAF7 text: 12.8:1 — AAA
Champagne Gold
#B5904E
Eyebrow labels, thin divider lines, quote marks, subtle accents. Matt, warm — not shiny or glittery.
On white: 3.2:1 — decorative use only
Warm Grey
#8C8876
Subtext, captions, attribution, placeholder text. Warm — never cool grey.
On white: 3.8:1 — captions only ≥14px
Cream
#F2EDE5
Card backgrounds, subtle section breaks, testimonial container. Warm creme — feels linen, not paper.
With #1A1A18 text: 15.1:1 — AAA
Usage Rules

Green: Backgrounds only when intentional (hero, 1 dark accent section). Text: only on light BG. Never as decorative highlight.
Gold: Lines, eyebrows, quote-marks only. Never fill area larger than 4px line.
White (#FFF): Card surfaces only — never as page background (use #FAFAF7).
Forbidden: Rose gold, sage green, cool grey, pure #000000, any neon variation.

Section 03

Spacing & Grid

8px base grid. All spacing values are multiples of 8.

8px — sp-1
Tight gaps, inline spacing
16px — sp-2
Label → element, icon gaps
24px — sp-3
Related elements within a block
32px — sp-4
Card padding, grouped items
48px — sp-6
Headline → body text, intra-section
64px — sp-8
Between major content blocks
80px — sp-10
Section padding desktop
120px — sp-15
Large section padding, hero content
Max Widths

Content (images, full-bleed): 1200px max-width, centered
Text sections (body copy, headlines): 900px max-width — forces readable line lengths
Narrow text (captions, attributions): 600px max-width
Mobile padding: 24px horizontal on all viewports ≤ 768px

Section 04

Buttons

On White / Cream Background
Primary — Hover: lighter green
Secondary — Hover: fills green
Ghost — Hover: gold underline slides in
On Dark Green Background
Primary on dark — Champagne Gold
Secondary on dark — ghost white border
Ghost on dark — gold underline
Button Rules

All button text: DM Sans, 11–12px, font-weight 400, letter-spacing 0.14–0.16em, uppercase.
Padding: 16px vertical × 36px horizontal (primary/secondary) — touch target min 44px.
No border-radius — sharp corners only. No shadows. No gradients.
Primary CTA: always dark green. Never gold as button fill on white BG.
